THE JOURNEY

10+ Years of Educational Leadership in Nashik

Founded under the visionary stewardship of Shri. Sachin Deore, Deore Academy started with a small classroom of 20 passionate aspirants near Ashok Stambh, Nashik. Over the last decade, we have grown into one of North Maharashtra's most celebrated centers for civil services coaching.

We dismantled the myth that students from Nashik, Dhule, and Jalgaon must endure the high costs and impersonal crowds of Delhi or Pune to clear UPSC or MPSC. With our personalized feedback loop, high-yield notes, and disciplined study rooms, our students routinely secure State Rank 1 and top All-India ranks.
